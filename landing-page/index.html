How it works

One add-on. Total upgrade.

Attaches to any standard manual wheelchair in three modular layers — no tools, no permanent changes.

01
Clip it on

The entire SmartDrive Assist system mounts onto your existing frame — clamp-based, fully reversible, compatible with all standard manual chairs. Zero modifications required.

02
Sensors take over

IR and ultrasonic sensors constantly scan the environment — detecting obstacles, drop-offs, and incline angles — feeding real-time data to the onboard controller.

03
You stay in control

Switch between electric and manual modes anytime. Use voice commands, the Bluetooth remote, or the high-contrast UI — while SmartDrive Assist handles safety automatically.

Overview

SmartDrive Assist at a glance.
Built for impact.

SmartDrive Assist is a student-built assistive tech initiative under TechCare Warriors — applying real hardware engineering to a problem that affects 75 million people worldwide.

The project sits at the intersection of servo robotics, inclusive design, and social impact — currently in active prototype development with interest from disability-tech organisations.

Hardware

Real Engineering

Servo motors, IR and ultrasonic sensors, dynamo recharging — engineered components, not just concepts.

Design

Inclusive by Default

Works with existing chairs. No user gets excluded based on what they already own.

Impact

SDG Aligned

Directly supports UN SDG 3 — Good Health and SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities.

Team

Student-Led

Conceived, designed, and prototyped by a student team from Gurugram, India.

Status

Where we are right now

SmartDrive Assist is currently in active prototype development. We have received encouraging feedback from leaders at BraveHeart Soldier Foundation and AssisTech Foundation, and are working toward a fully field-testable version of the system.

Our Journey

How SmartDrive Assist became our mission.

We are Ivaan Singh (PYP 4), Siraaj Singh Pabby (PYP 4), and Utsa Banerjee (PYP 5) from Pathways School Gurgaon.

1Empathise

Spotted a mobility gap: we saw wheelchair users struggle and decided to help.

2Define

Understood the problem: we listened, researched daily wheelchair challenges, and focused on practical support users actually need.

3Platform

Codeavour 7.0 gave us a stage to build and spread awareness.

4Ideate

Built the solution: SmartDrive Assist.

5Validation & Prototype

Validated with founders/CEOs and, most importantly, real wheelchair users.

6Testing & Implementation

In progress: testing the prototype, refining implementation, and preparing for wider real-world use.
